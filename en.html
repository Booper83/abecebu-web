Abecebú

A game for learning letters and numbers in Spanish, ages 2 to 6

In short: Abecebú collects no data. It has no ads, no analytics and no in-app purchases, and it does not need an internet connection. Everything it saves stays on your device.

Privacy policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026.

Data we collect

None. The app sends no information to us or to anyone else: it does not connect to the internet.

What is saved on the device

To work, the app saves the following on the device itself, and only there:

  • The player profiles you create: a name or nickname, an age and a picture. The real name is not needed.
  • The settings for each profile and each game.
  • The stickers earned in the album.
  • The adult PIN, saved in a form that cannot be read back.

None of this leaves the device. It is deleted when the app is uninstalled, and profiles can be deleted one by one from the adult settings.

Ads, analytics and third parties

The app shows no ads, does not measure how it is used and includes no software from other companies that collects data. There are no in-app purchases and no links to other websites or social networks.

Permissions

The app does not ask for access to the camera, microphone, location, contacts or photos.

Children

Abecebú is made for young children. The adult settings are protected by a question and a PIN. Because no data is collected, no data from children is collected either.

Changes to this policy

If the policy changes, the new version will be published here with its new date.

Support and contact

If you have a question, have found a problem or want to ask about privacy, write to romualdooliver@gmail.com.
